Summary

Razorpay is a Bengaluru-based fintech company founded in 2014 [1][2]. The company has completed a reverse flip, shifting its parent domicile and global headquarters back to India [3]. Across multiple financing rounds, Razorpay has raised approximately $816.6 million to $822 million [1].

Product & business model

Razorpay operates a diversified fintech revenue model spanning transaction fees, subscription plans, and value-added solutions [1]. Its product suite includes payouts, payroll, neobanking, loans, and business banking workflows [1].

Team

The company was founded by Harshil Mathur and Shashank Kumar [1]. Harshil Mathur serves as the CEO and Co-Founder [1]. Razorpay currently has an employee headcount of approximately 4,027 to 4,042 [1].

Funding & traction

Razorpay has secured total funding in the range of $816.6 million to $822 million across multiple investment rounds [1]. Its organizational footprint spans 4,027 to 4,042 employees [1].
