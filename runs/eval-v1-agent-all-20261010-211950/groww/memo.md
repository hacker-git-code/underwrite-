# Groww

**dig deeper – strong product traction and sizable funding but notable regulatory settlements raise compliance risk** (confidence: medium)

## Summary
Groww, founded in 2016 [C1], is a Bangalore‑area (Bellandur, Panathur, Karnataka) based fintech platform that offers a suite of financial services to Indian investors [C5][C2]. The company is led by CEO Lalit Keshre [C3] and employs 3,155 staff members [C6]. Groww has raised $1.33 billion in total funding [C4] and its parent firm now commands a market capitalisation above $11.3 billion [C10]. The firm filed its Red Herring Prospectus with SEBI to launch an IPO worth ₹6,632.30 crore scheduled for 4 Nov 2025 [C12][C8][C13].

## Product & business model
Groww operates as a multi‑product platform delivering brokerage, mutual funds, digital gold and other investment products, positioning itself as a trusted financial partner for Indians [C5]. Its technology stack includes ReactJS for the web app and Flutter/React Native for mobile, with Redux or Provider for state management [C11].
