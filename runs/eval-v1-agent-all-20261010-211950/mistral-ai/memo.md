## Summary
Mistral AI, founded in 2023 in Paris, has raised €1.7 billion in a Series‬C round and counts Cisco and European government agencies among its customers, positioning it as a European contender against OpenAI, Anthropic and Cohere [C1][C2][C5][C7][C8].

## Product & business model
Mistral AI offers the Forge platform that lets customers customize AI models for specific data and use‑cases [C6]. It differentiates through open‑weight models such as \"Chonk\", emphasizing performance and security [C11]. Partnerships (e.g., with Capgemini) enable secure, scalable generative AI solutions [C12].

## Team

## Funding & traction
