## Summary
Postman was founded in 2014 [C1] and is headquartered in San Francisco, California, United States [C2]. The company was founded by Abhinav Asthana, Ankit Sobti, and Abhijit Kane [C3] and is led by CEO Abhinav Asthana [C4]. It has raised a total of $433,000,000 in funding [C5], with the most recent round being a $225,000,000 Series D on August 18, 2021 [C6]. Postman reports 35 million users across 500,000 organizations, including 98% of the Fortune 500 [C8]. The API platform is monetized via tiered SaaS subscription plans [C7][C14]. The API management market is projected to exceed $146 billion by 2034 [C16].

## Product & business model
Postman offers an API platform for building, testing, and managing APIs across the lifecycle, delivered through tiered SaaS subscription plans [C7][C14]. In late 2025 the company announced enhancements for enterprise readiness and AI‑ready API development [C10]. In 2024 it released its sixth annual “State of the API Report” and launched Postman v11 [C12].
