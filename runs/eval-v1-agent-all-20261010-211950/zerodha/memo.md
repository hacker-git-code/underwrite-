# Zerodha

**dig deeper – strong growth but regulatory and competitive risks warrant further investigation** (confidence: medium)

## Summary
Zerodha is a bootstrapped discount brokerage founded in 2010 that offers zero brokerage on a wide range of financial products and has grown to about 8.1 million active users with revenue around ₹4,200 crore in FY‥2025【C2】【C1】【C9】. It operates from Bengaluru, India and faces increasing competition and regulatory headwinds【C8】【C7】.

## Product & business model
Zerodha provides zero‑brokerage trading for equity delivery, futures & options, commodities, currencies, direct mutual funds, fixed deposits, NPS and its own Varsity education platform【C1】. This zero‑fee, technology‑driven model positions it as a discount broker in India’s retail investing market【C1】.

## Team
The company was co‑founded by brothers Nithin Kamath and Nikhil Kamath【C4】. Nithin Kamath serves as Founder and CEO【C5】. The firm remains 100 % founder‑owned and has raised no external venture capital【C6】.

## Funding & traction
Zerodha has raised zero external funding, reflecting a fully bootstrapped capital structure【C6】. By late 2024/early 2025 it served roughly 8.1 million active users and generated approximately ₹4,200 crore in revenue/profit metrics【C9】.

## Market & competition
India’s discount brokerage market is increasingly contested. Groww captured 26.6 % market share with 13.16 million active users in late 2024, directly competing with Zerodha and Angel One【C7】. Competitive pressure may affect user acquisition and pricing dynamics.
