# Deel

**Strong interest due to rapid revenue scale exceeding $1B ARR, substantial capital reserves, and broad global HR/payroll platform capabilities.** (confidence: high)

## Summary
Deel is a global payroll and HR platform founded in 2019 and headquartered in San Francisco, California [C1][C2][C6]. The company has demonstrated exceptional growth, reaching over a $1 billion revenue run rate in 2025 and serving more than 40,000 customers globally [C10][C12][C13].

## Product & business model
Deel provides a comprehensive platform for global payroll, HR, benefits, mobility, performance, and device management [C6]. The company has expanded its capabilities via an active acquisition strategy, including earmarking substantial capital for M&A and acquiring Boundless in 2025 [C9][C15].

## Team
Deel was founded by Alex Bouaziz and Shuo Wang [C3]. Alex Bouaziz serves as the company's CEO [C4]. The company operates at significant scale with approximately 9,674 employees [C7].

## Funding & traction
Deel has raised a total of $1,281,999,396 in funding [C5]. In Q1 2025, Deel announced reaching a $1 billion annual revenue run rate, later reporting over $1.5 billion ARR as of September 2026 while serving 40,000+ customers [C10][C11][C12][C13].

## Market & competition
Deel operates in the global HR and payroll software market [C6]. Its primary competitors include Rippling, Papaya Global, and Remote [C8].
