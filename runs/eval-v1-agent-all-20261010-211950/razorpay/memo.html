Summary

Razorpay is an Indian digital payments infrastructure company founded in 2014 and headquartered in Bengaluru (Adugodi), India.21 Led by CEO and Co-Founder Harshil Mathur, the company has scaled to roughly 4,042 employees and commands an estimated 21% share of the domestic payment gateway market.24

Product & business model

Razorpay's primary product is a payment gateway that allows businesses to process transactions via credit and debit cards, UPI, and net banking.3 Its business model relies on charging a standard 2% platform fee plus 18% GST on transactions where merchant discount rates (MDR) are zero (such as UPI), alongside standard take rates/MDR for card payments and net banking.6

Team

Harshil Mathur leads the company as CEO and Co-Founder.2 The company maintains a total headcount of approximately 4,042 employees.2

Funding & traction

Razorpay has raised between $816.6 million and $822 million in aggregate funding from investors such as Lightspeed, FinSight Ventures, and Axevil Capital.27 Its last recorded financing event was a $75 million secondary market round on May 10, 2022.2 The company's valuation reached a peak official level of $7.5 billion during its December 2021 Series F round, with later reports citing a $9.2 billion valuation in June 2025.89 Razorpay serves over 450,000 customers and generates an annualized Total Payment Volume (TPV) of $180 billion.10 Operationally, revenue grew from Rs 2,279 crore in FY23 to ₹2,501.4 crore with ₹33.5 crore in net profit in FY24, and reached Rs 3,783 crore in FY25 representing a 65% year-over-year increase.111213

Market & competition

Razorpay holds the top position in the Indian payment gateway landscape with an estimated 21% market share.4 It operates in a competitive environment alongside players such as PayU, Cashfree, CCAvenue, and Paytm.4

Risks & red flags

The company has experienced regulatory scrutiny in the past; specifically, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) subjected Razorpay to a merchant-onboarding ban that was lifted in December 2023.5
