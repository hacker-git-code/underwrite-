# Razorpay

**Dig deeper given Razorpay's dominant 21% market share, strong revenue growth, and profitability, while evaluating regulatory dynamics following the lifted RBI restrictions.** (confidence: medium)

## Summary
Razorpay is an Indian digital payments infrastructure company founded in 2014 and headquartered in Bengaluru (Adugodi), India [C1][C2][C7]. Led by CEO and Co-Founder Harshil Mathur, the company has scaled to roughly 4,042 employees and commands an estimated 21% share of the domestic payment gateway market [C3][C6][C14].

## Product & business model
Razorpay's primary product is a payment gateway that allows businesses to process transactions via credit and debit cards, UPI, and net banking [C12]. Its business model relies on charging a standard 2% platform fee plus 18% GST on transactions where merchant discount rates (MDR) are zero (such as UPI), alongside standard take rates/MDR for card payments and net banking [C18].

## Team
Harshil Mathur leads the company as CEO and Co-Founder [C3]. The company maintains a total headcount of approximately 4,042 employees [C6].

## Funding & traction
Razorpay has raised between $816.6 million and $822 million in aggregate funding from investors such as Lightspeed, FinSight Ventures, and Axevil Capital [C4][C9]. Its last recorded financing event was a $75 million secondary market round on May 10, 2022 [C5]. The company's valuation reached a peak official level of $7.5 billion during its December 2021 Series F round, with later reports citing a $9.2 billion valuation in June 2025 [C8][C17]. Razorpay serves over 450,000 customers and generates an annualized Total Payment Volume (TPV) of $180 billion [C11]. Operationally, revenue grew from Rs 2,279 crore in FY23 to ₹2,501.4 crore with ₹33.5 crore in net profit in FY24, and reached Rs 3,783 crore in FY25 representing a 65% year-over-year increase [C10][C15][C16].

## Market & competition
Razorpay holds the top position in the Indian payment gateway landscape with an estimated 21% market share [C14]. It operates in a competitive environment alongside players such as PayU, Cashfree, CCAvenue, and Paytm [C14].

## Risks & red flags
The company has experienced regulatory scrutiny in the past; specifically, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) subjected Razorpay to a merchant-onboarding ban that was lifted in December 2023 [C13].
