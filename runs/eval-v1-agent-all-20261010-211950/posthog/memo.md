## Product & business model
PostHog provides a product suite comprising product analytics, session replay, and experiments [C10]. Its source code is publicly accessible on GitHub, with a majority under an MIT license alongside separate licenses for other parts [C12]. PostHog offers self-hosting via a free Docker Compose deployment under an MIT license [C7]. The platform employs a freemium model featuring a monthly free allowance per product, including 1M analytics events, 5K session replays, and 1M feature flag requests [C8]. PostHog released PostHog Desktop in early 2026 and announced a partnership with Stripe in March 2026 [C6][C11].
