# Supabase

**Strong interest due to massive developer adoption reaching nearly 10 million users and an estimated $170 million ARR, though reliability and infrastructure risks warrant close review.** (confidence: high)

## Summary
Supabase is an open-source Backend-as-a-Service (BaaS) provider founded in 2020 [C1][C10]. The company has achieved substantial scale, reporting nearly 10 million developers on the platform by June 2026 and an estimated $170 million in ARR by May 2026 [C8][C9].

## Product & business model
Supabase provides an open-source PostgreSQL-based alternative to Firebase [C10]. Its core product suite includes a Postgres database, Authentication, instant APIs, Realtime, Functions, Storage, and Vector embeddings [C5]. Recent product additions include the Supabase Pipelines public alpha, Sign in with ChatGPT beta, and Unified Logs beta [C11].

## Team
Supabase is led by CEO Paul Copplestone [C2]. The company has a reported headcount of 439 employees [C4].

## Funding & traction
Supabase raised a $100 million Series E round at a $5 billion valuation in October 2025 [C6]. Total capital raised is reported at approximately $1.04 billion to $1.19 billion, with valuations reported between $5 billion and $10.5 billion [C3][C6][C7]. Platform adoption reached nearly 10 million developers by June 2026 [C8]. Sacra estimated Supabase's ARR at approximately $170 million as of May 2026 [C9].

## Market & competition
Supabase operates in the Backend-as-a-Service market and primarily competes against Google's Firebase as an open-source PostgreSQL alternative [C10].

## Risks & red flags
Service reliability is a key risk, highlighted by a major outage lasting 3 hours and 42 minutes that prevented users from accessing their Postgres databases, Auth, and Data APIs [C12]. Additionally, secondary data providers reflect conflicting totals for funding and company valuation [C3][C6][C7].
