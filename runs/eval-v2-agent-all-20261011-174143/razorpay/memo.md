## Product & business model
Razorpay offers a full‑stack suite of payment and financial products for Indian merchants, including payment gateway, neobanking, invoicing and checkout solutions, positioning itself as a full‑stack financial solutions provider [C7]. It is PCI‑DSS Level 1 certified and complies with RBI regulations [C9] and provides a direct integration app for Shopify stores [C10].
