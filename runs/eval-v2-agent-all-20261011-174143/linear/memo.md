# Linear

**Strong interest given Linear's unicorn valuation, strong customer traction exceeding 20,000 paid businesses, and established product leadership against legacy incumbents.** (confidence: high)

## Summary
Linear was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in San Francisco, California [C1][C2]. The company offers a modern project management platform and has achieved a $1.25 billion valuation following an $82 million Series C funding round [C9]. Led by co-founder and CEO Karri Saarinen, Linear has reached over 20,000 paid business customers while expanding into large enterprise contracts [C3][C10].

## Product & business model
Linear operates a per-user subscription model offering Free, Basic, Business, and Enterprise tiers [C8]. The platform continues to expand developer workflow capabilities, introducing features such as stacked pull requests and mobile document editing in late 2026 [C12].

## Team
Linear was co-founded by CEO Karri Saarinen, CTO Tuomas Artman, and co-founder Jori Lallo [C3][C4][C5]. Company headcount is reported at 118 team members by leadership, while company directory records list an employee count of 291 [C7][C10].

## Funding & traction
Linear has raised a total of $233,200,000 in funding across its rounds [C6]. Most recently, Linear closed an $82 million Series C round led by Accel at a $1.25 billion valuation [C9]. The business reports over 20,000 paid business customers and strong ARR growth driven by large enterprise accounts spending $100k or more [C10].

## Market & competition
Linear competes directly in the agile project management space, where Jira serves as a primary competitor supported by an established enterprise footprint and a deep ecosystem [C11].

## Risks & red flags
Jira poses significant market competition and incumbent risk due to its extensive enterprise ecosystem and entrenched market presence [C13]. Additionally, there is a discrepancy in recorded team size, with internal leadership reporting 118 team members and external company records indicating 291 employees [C7][C10].
