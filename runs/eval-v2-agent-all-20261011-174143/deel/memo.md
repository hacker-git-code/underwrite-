# Deel

**strong interest – the company shows rapid growth, profitability and a large market opportunity, though recent legal disputes warrant monitoring** (confidence: high)

## Summary
Deel, founded in 2019 [C1], is headquartered in San Francisco, California, United States [C2]. The company is led by Co‑Founder and CEO Alex Bouaziz [C3][C7]. It has raised $1.28 billion in total funding [C4] and most recently closed a $300 million Series E round in October 2025 at a $17.3 billion valuation [C9][C17]. Deel achieved a $1 billion revenue run‑rate in June 2025 and reported $800 million ARR with 70 % growth, becoming profitable in Q3 2023 [C12][C13][C14]. The platform serves over 35,000 customers and 1.5 million workers across 150+ countries, processing $22 billion in annual payroll [C11][C6].

## Product & business model
Deel provides a cloud‑based platform that handles payroll, HR, benefits, mobility, performance, and device management for organizations operating in more than 150 countries [C6]. (No explicit claim about the underlying business model is available.)

## Team
Alex Bouaziz is the Co‑Founder and CEO [C3][C7]. The company was founded by Alex Bouaziz, Shuo Wang, and Ofer Simon [C8].

## Funding & traction
Total equity raised to date: $1,281,999,396 [C4]. Latest financing: $300 million Series E round co‑led by Ribbit Capital and Andreessen Horowitz in October 2025 [C9][C17]. Earlier, the company had raised $631 million as of October 2021 [C10]. Revenue milestones include a $1 billion run‑rate (June 2025) [C12] and $800 million ARR with 70 % growth (early 2025) [C13]. The firm has been profitable since Q3 2023 [C14]. Customer base exceeds 35,000 organizations and 1.5 million workers, processing $22 billion in payroll annually [C11].

## Market & competition
Deel competes with Native Teams, Rippling, Pebl (formerly Velocity Global), Oyster, Multiplier, and Remote [C15]. Its global coverage of 150+ countries and large payroll processing volume position it strongly in the rapidly expanding HR‑tech market [C6][C11].

## Risks & red flags
