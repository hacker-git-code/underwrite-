# Zerodha

**dig deeper: strong market position but zero funding, need to validate sustainability** (confidence: high)

## Summary
Zerodha, founded in 2010, is India's largest discount broker with over 1.7 crore customers and an estimated annual revenue between $500 M and $1 B [C1][C7][C13]. It is fully bootstrapped, having raised no external capital [C4][C8].

## Product & business model
Zerodha offers low‑cost equity and derivatives trading and provides the developer‑first Kite Connect APIs for custom trading workflows [C12]. Its business model centers on a discount brokerage model supplemented by API‑driven services.

## Team
The company was founded by Nithin Kamath and Nikhil Kamath, with Nithin Kamath serving as CEO [C5][C6].

## Funding & traction
Zero venture‑capital funding and no financing rounds to date [C4][C8]; revenue estimates between $500 M‑$1 B indicate strong traction [C13].

## Market & competition
It holds the top position by net worth on the NSE and competes with discount brokers Groww, Angel One, and Upstox [C7][C9].

## Risks & red flags
Recent SEBI regulatory changes, such as limits on weekly expiries, could impact trading volumes and revenues [C11].
