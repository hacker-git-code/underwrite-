## Summary
PostHog was founded in 2020 and is headquartered in San Francisco, California, United States [C1][C2]. The company has raised a total of $182.15M in funding, most recently a $75M Series E round on September 29 2025, valuing the company at $1.4B [C4][C5][C9]. Its team comprises 224 employees and operates fully remotely across more than 20 countries [C3][C8].
