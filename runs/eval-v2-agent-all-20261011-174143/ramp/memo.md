## Product & business model
Ramp generates revenue primarily through its Ramp card and Ramp Plus subscription offerings【C7】. Recent financials show revenue growth from $500M to over $1B within a 12‑month period【C8】.

## Team
The company was founded by Eric Glyman, Karim Atiyeh, and Gene Lee【C5】. Co‑founders Karim Atiyeh and Eric Glyman serve as co‑CEOs【C6】.

## Funding & traction

## Market & competition
