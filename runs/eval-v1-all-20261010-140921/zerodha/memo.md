## Summary
Zerodha is an Indian discount brokerage founded in 2010 by Nithin Kamath and Nikhil Kamath [C1][C4]. Headquartered in Bengaluru, India, the company maintains a bootstrapped structure with zero external funding rounds raised [C2][C3][C7]. It operates at massive scale, serving millions of active clients and reporting substantial profitability [C8][C12][C13].

## Product & business model
Zerodha operates a low-cost brokerage model offering brokerage-free equity investments as well as retail and institutional broking [C6]. The company generates revenue by charging fees of up to ₹20 per order for intraday and futures and options (F&O) trades [C6].
