## Summary
Supabase, founded in 2020, offers an open‑source Postgres‑based development platform as an alternative to Firebase and has raised over $1.18 B in funding, most recently a $150 M round on 2026‑10‑02 [C2][C3][C6][C7]. ## Product & business model
Supabase provides a managed Postgres database with authentication, instant APIs, realtime subscriptions, functions, storage, and vector embeddings, positioning itself as an open‑source Firebase alternative [C1][C9].
