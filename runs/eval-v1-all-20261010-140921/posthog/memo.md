# PostHog

## Summary
PostHog is a developer-focused software company founded in 2020 and headquartered in San Francisco, California, United States [C2][C3][C4]. Co-founded and led by co-CEOs James Hawkins and Tim Glaser, the company provides an open-source analytics platform and has scaled to 224 employees with over $182 million in funding [C1][C5][C6][C7][C9].

## Product & business model
PostHog provides an integrated open-source platform combining product analytics, session recording, web analytics, feature flags, and A/B testing [C1]. In addition, the platform has expanded capabilities to include proactive bug fixes and pull request generation with agents [C11]. PostHog monetizes through a usage-based or transparent monetization model featuring generous free tiers and inbound-led sales [C1].

## Team
PostHog was co-founded by James Hawkins and Tim Glaser [C5]. Both Hawkins and Glaser currently serve as co-CEOs [C6]. The company operates with a headcount of 224 employees [C9].

## Funding & traction
PostHog has raised a total of $182,150,000 in capital [C7]. Its latest financing was a $75,000,000 Series E round led by Peak XV Partners on September 29, 2025 [C8]. The organization has grown to an employee headcount of 224 [C9].

## Market & competition
PostHog operates in the analytics and session replay space, competing directly against established tools including Mixpanel, Amplitude, Fullstory, Heap, and Google Analytics [C10].
