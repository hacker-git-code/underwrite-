## Summary
Razorpay is an Indian payments and financial services provider founded in 2014 and headquartered in Adugodi, Karnataka, India [C2][C3]. The company leads India's payment gateway sector with an estimated 21% market share and has secured $816,600,000 USD in total funding [C6][C9].

## Product & business model
Razorpay generates revenue primarily via transaction fees, subscription plans, and value-added fintech offerings such as payouts, payroll, neobanking, and loans [C1]. In partnership with IndusInd Bank, the company introduced 'RAY', a conversational AI account manager deployed on WhatsApp for business clients [C10]. Furthermore, Ezetap Mobile Solutions Private Limited completed a merger with Razorpay Payments Private Limited, operationalized from October 1, 2025 [C11].

## Team
Razorpay was founded by Harshil Mathur and Shashank Kumar [C4]. Harshil Mathur holds the role of CEO and Co-Founder [C5]. The organization employs approximately 4,042 people [C7].

## Funding & traction
Razorpay has raised a total of $816,600,000 USD in funding [C6]. In terms of market traction, it leads the payment gateway space with an estimated 21% market share [C9].

## Market & competition
Within India's payment landscape, Razorpay competes against PayU, Cashfree Payments, CCAvenue, Paytm Payment Gateway, and PhonePe [C8].
