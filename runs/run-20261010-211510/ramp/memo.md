# Ramp

**dig deeper – impressive growth and funding but recent congressional investigation warrants closer review** (confidence: medium)

## Summary
Ramp was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in New York, United States [C1][C2]. The company is led by co‑CEOs Eric Glyman and Karim Atiyeh [C4]. Since inception, Ramp has raised over $3.22 billion in financing and most recently closed a $1.85 billion round in October 2026 at a $60 billion valuation [C5][C11][C15]. By June 2026 the firm reported more than 70,000 customers, over $1 billion in annualized revenue and $200 billion in annualized purchase volume [C9][C12].

## Product & business model
Ramp offers an integrated spend‑management platform that combines corporate cards, travel booking, expense management and accounts‑payable capabilities [C7][C14]. Its revenue model is anchored on card interchange fees and subscription fees for the Ramp Plus software suite [C8].

## Team
The founding team comprises Eric Glyman, Karim Atiyeh and Gene Lee [C3]. Glyman and Atiyeh serve as co‑CEOs, driving both product vision and go‑to‑market strategy [C4].

## Funding & traction
Total funding to date exceeds $3.22 billion across multiple rounds, with participation from Dragoneer Investment Group, Thrive Capital and Founders Fund in the latest financing [C5][C6]. The October 2026 round raised $1.85 billion, valuing the company at roughly $60 billion [C11][C15]. Ramp’s customer base has surpassed 70 k and its annualized revenue tops $1 billion, indicating strong market adoption [C9][C12].

## Market & competition
Ramp operates in the corporate spend‑management space, competing with firms such as Brex, Bill.com, Expensify, SAP Concur, Airbase, Navan, American Express and Corpay [C10].

## Risks & red flags
In May 2025 a U.S. Congressman launched an investigation into Ramp’s bid for a $25 million federal contract, citing concerns about the company’s experience and investor connections [C13]. This regulatory scrutiny could pose reputational and operational risks.
