## Product & business model
Vercel monetizes via usage‑based pricing on compute, bandwidth and storage, plus per‑seat Pro subscriptions [C9][C16]. Its platform is optimized for zero‑config Next.js deployments, making it the top choice when speed‑to‑cutover matters [C11].
