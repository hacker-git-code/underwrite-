Summary

Razorpay is an India-based financial technology company founded in 2014 by Harshil Mathur and Shashank Kumar [1][1][1]. The company has raised approximately $816.6 million in total funding and employs over 4,000 people [1][1].

Product & business model

Razorpay generates revenue through a fee-based structure consisting of transaction fees, premium subscriptions, and additional financial services [2]. The company introduced Vulcan, India's first AI payments foundation model trained on 4 billion transactions [3]. Additionally, at Razorpay Sprint 2026, the company unveiled more than 100 product launches spanning AI-native payment improvements and agentic business banking [4].

Team

The company was founded by Harshil Mathur and Shashank Kumar [1]. Harshil Mathur serves as the current CEO [1]. The organization maintains a headcount exceeding 4,000 staff members [1].

Funding & traction

Razorpay has accumulated approximately $816.6 million in total capital raised [1]. The firm has also demonstrated aggressive M&A traction, completing 7 acquisitions across the payments, cybersecurity, and HRTech sectors [5].

Market & competition

Razorpay's key competitors in the digital payments landscape include Cashfree Payments, PayU India, CCAvenue, BillDesk, Paytm Payment Gateway, and Juspay [6].

Risks & red flags

The company operates in a highly contested market against several established domestic payment processors and gateways [6].
