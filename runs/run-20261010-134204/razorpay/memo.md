# Razorpay

**Strong interest given Razorpay's massive scale, $816.6 million in funding, and expansion into AI payments, though regulatory standing and IPO readiness warrant deeper review.**

## Summary
Razorpay is an India-based financial technology company founded in 2014 by Harshil Mathur and Shashank Kumar [C1][C3][C4]. The company has raised approximately $816.6 million in total funding and employs over 4,000 people [C7][C8].

## Product & business model
Razorpay generates revenue through a fee-based structure consisting of transaction fees, premium subscriptions, and additional financial services [C6]. The company introduced Vulcan, India's first AI payments foundation model trained on 4 billion transactions [C10]. Additionally, at Razorpay Sprint 2026, the company unveiled more than 100 product launches spanning AI-native payment improvements and agentic business banking [C12].

## Team
The company was founded by Harshil Mathur and Shashank Kumar [C4]. Harshil Mathur serves as the current CEO [C5]. The organization maintains a headcount exceeding 4,000 staff members [C8].

## Funding & traction
Razorpay has accumulated approximately $816.6 million in total capital raised [C7]. The firm has also demonstrated aggressive M&A traction, completing 7 acquisitions across the payments, cybersecurity, and HRTech sectors [C11].

## Market & competition
Razorpay's key competitors in the digital payments landscape include Cashfree Payments, PayU India, CCAvenue, BillDesk, Paytm Payment Gateway, and Juspay [C9].
